# Forklift Training Classes

Forklift training classes teach safe operation and help employers meet OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178. You can take **online classes** for the theory portion or **in-person classes** at a local school—both still need a workplace practical evaluation.

**Fastest common path in 2026:** finish OSHA-aligned online theory for **$49** (often under 60 minutes), download your certificate, then have your employer complete the hands-on eval.

## What OSHA requires from forklift training classes

Under **OSHA Standard 29 CFR 1910.178**, employers must ensure operators get:

- **Formal instruction** — classroom or online covering rules, hazards, and procedures  
- **Practical training** — demonstration and practice  
- **Evaluation** — confirmed safe operation in the actual workplace  

Refresher training is required at least every three years, or sooner after an accident, near-miss, poor performance, or a major equipment/workplace change.

## What a good class covers

Quality forklift training classes should include:

- Pre-operation inspection  
- Safe operating techniques  
- Load handling and stability  
- Pedestrian safety and traffic rules  
- Hazard recognition  
- Equipment limits and capabilities  
- Refueling / battery / charging basics as applicable  
- Emergency procedures  

### Practical evaluation component

Theory can be online; OSHA still requires a hands-on evaluation. Choose a program that gives your employer a clear evaluation checklist.

## How to choose a forklift training class

1. Confirm **OSHA 1910.178** coverage and clear documentation  
2. Match **truck class** to what you’ll operate  
3. Confirm how the **practical evaluation** will be done at work  
4. Compare **time and cost** — online theory is usually fastest for multi-shift teams  
5. Prefer providers with mobile access and employer-ready checklists  

## Industry notes

- **Warehouses** — electric riders, reach trucks, narrow aisles, pedestrian traffic  
- **Construction** — rough-terrain trucks, outdoor surfaces, other equipment nearby  
- **Manufacturing** — mixed truck types, attachments, production-floor traffic  

## Refresher training and recertification

Schedule refresher training before the three-year mark. Online refresher theory plus a new workplace evaluation is a common approach for returning operators.
