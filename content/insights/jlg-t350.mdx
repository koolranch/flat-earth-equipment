The JLG T350 is a trailer-towable articulating boom in JLG's Tow-Pro line. It exists for the job a truck-mounted or self-propelled boom cannot reach economically: a single lift at a site with no flatbed, no crane, and no room to store a 15,000 lb machine. You hook it to a pickup, tow it at highway speed, drop the outriggers, and work.

## T350 vs ET350 vs T500J

- **ET350** is the same boom with a 24V battery-electric drive and an on-board charger. Choose it for indoor work, noise-restricted sites, or where fuel handling is a problem. Cycle count per charge is the trade-off.
- **T500J** adds a jib and reaches 50 ft platform height with roughly the same footprint on the trailer. It is heavier and needs a larger tow vehicle.
- **T350** is the general-purpose pick: gas power means no charge planning, and 34 ft covers two-story exterior work, tree trimming, signage, and most light-commercial maintenance.

## Setup and operating notes

The T350 works only on outriggers. Auto-leveling handles the fine adjustment, but the machine still needs firm ground inside its outrigger footprint (about 10 ft 5 in wide); on soft ground use pads. Outrigger interlocks prevent boom function until all four are set and the chassis is level, so a boom that will not lift on a freshly delivered unit is usually an outrigger or level sensor issue, not a hydraulic one.

Because the T350 is Group B under ANSI A92 (the platform can extend past the tipping line), operators need boom-lift training and a harness tied to the platform anchor. See [MEWP training requirements](/insights/mobile-elevating-work-platform-training).

## Wear items and common faults

On rental-fleet T350s the recurring parts are:

- **Level and tilt sensors**: the outrigger auto-level and boom interlocks depend on them, and they fail from vibration and water intrusion.
- **Platform control box and joystick**: the detachable controller takes abuse during transport.
- **Outrigger limit switches and solenoids**: mud and corrosion at ground level.
- **Charger** (electric units) and **12V battery** (gas units): a flat battery is the most common "won't start" call.
- **Tires and trailer lighting**: highway-speed towing wears tires faster than most owners expect; check pressure before every tow.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a JLG T350 weigh?**
Roughly 3,900–4,100 lb depending on options, which keeps it within the towing rating of a half-ton pickup with a standard 2 in ball.

**Can the T350 drive under its own power?**
No. Tow-Pro booms are moved by towing only; on site they are positioned by hand or with the tow vehicle before the outriggers are set.

**Is the T350 electric or gas?**
Current-production T350 units have an 11.7 hp gasoline engine. The ET350 is the battery-electric version, and some older T350 units were 24V DC. Check the hood and the data plate.

**Does the T350 need a special license to tow?**
Not in most states; it is well under the 10,000 lb GVWR threshold. Check your state's trailer brake and lighting requirements.

**Can I use the T350 indoors?**
Only the electric ET350 is suitable for enclosed spaces. Do not run the gasoline unit indoors.
