Tennant 379609 is a hydraulic motor. The part we list for that number is an aftermarket replacement for Tennant floor-care equipment.

## The listing

The [Tennant 379609 hydraulic motor](/parts/tennant-379609-hydraulic-motor) is in stock at $1,739. That listing includes a 1-year warranty. Freight is calculated at checkout from the order value.

A listing is not a fit for every machine.

## What the motor does

The motor turns hydraulic pressure into rotation. On a floor-care machine that rotation drives a function such as the brushes or the traction circuit. A leak at the shaft, a scored housing, or gears that no longer turn are motor failures. A seal kit is a different part and will not repair a motor that has stopped turning.
